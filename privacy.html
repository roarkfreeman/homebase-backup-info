Privacy policy

HomeBase Backup Pilot
Effective October 31, 2026

Purpose and access

HomeBase Backup Pilot is a personal, owner-only backup utility using rclone. It accesses Google Drive file content and metadata only after its owner authorizes read-only access. It uses that data solely for the owner's personal backup process: copying owner-selected HomeBase data through the owner's PC to the owner's pCloud account.

Storage and retention

The process retains a local working cache, backup manifests, and backup versions. The owner manages retention and deletion of these copies. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing backups, caches, or manifests.

Data use

The utility does not sell data, use it for advertising, or use it for third-party AI training. Google Drive and pCloud process data as the owner's selected storage providers under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

This public website

The public information website displays no backed-up files and offers no visitor accounts or file access. These information pages contain no analytics or cookie-setting code. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs under its own privacy policy.

Control and contact

The owner can revoke access through Google Account permissions. For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the Google consent screen. Do not post private files or credentials publicly.
